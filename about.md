My name is Rushika Prasad, and I am currently pursuing a M. Eng. degree at Cornell University. Growing up, I observed a slow, collective loss of hope in robotics as the solution to humanity's problems. The days in which we envisioned robotics as an active and interactive part of our lives has been replaced with a preference for technology that hides its mechanical aspects. The rapid progress of robotics has forestalled development intention human-robot interactions. In my work, I have aimed to explore this space through multiple lenses, and this website is a collection of some of the projects I created from that discovery. 

My hobbies include Competitive Spanish Debate, rock climbing, ice skating, reading, and learning languages. 

If you have any projects, companies, or ideas related to product design, robotics or human-robotic interaction, I would love to hear from you at my email: rp548@cornell.edu.
